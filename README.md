### 🏙️ Bachelor's Thesis: Urban Growth of Mazar-e-Sharif (CA-Markov)

I analyzed urban expansion of Mazar-e-Sharif from 2003 to 2023 using Landsat imagery (ENVI) and simulated future growth with an integrated **Cellular Automata and Markov Chain** model (IDRISI TerrSet), with spatial analysis in ArcGIS.

| Year | Built-up area (ha) |
|---|---|
| 2003 | 1,318 |
| 2013 | 3,300.6 |
